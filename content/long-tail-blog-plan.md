# FixLeads Studio — long-tail content plan

Purpose: build useful topical coverage for UAE home-service businesses without publishing thin, repetitive location pages. Prioritise pages that answer a real buyer question and naturally link to the relevant industry page, pricing, contact form and local SEO pillar.

## Publishing rules
- Publish only after the article is genuinely written, reviewed and linked from the blog index.
- Do not claim first-hand results, customer counts, client stories or quotations without evidence and permission.
- Use the target phrase naturally in the title, H1, introduction and relevant subheadings; avoid keyword stuffing.
- Include a clear search intent, a useful original checklist or template, credible external references where relevant, and a next step.
- Use a canonical URL, unique meta title/description, descriptive social image, author/editorial review details where accurate, and valid Article/Breadcrumb structured data.
- Do not create a separate city page unless the business or topic has meaningful, unique information for that location.

## Suggested cadence
- Weeks 1–2: articles 1–4, one per core industry, with original page outlines and realistic examples.
- Weeks 3–4: articles 5–8, focused on service-area quality and conversion paths.
- Weeks 5–6: articles 9–12, measurement and local SEO education.
- Review Search Console query impressions and enquiry data after publication; revise the plan based on actual search demand rather than assuming every topic will rank.

## Editorial brief for every article
1. State who the article is for and what decision it helps them make.
2. Answer the core question early.
3. Include a concrete checklist, comparison, page outline or decision framework.
4. Explain limitations and exceptions; do not promise guaranteed rankings or leads.
5. Link to one relevant industry page, one supporting article and one conversion page.
6. Review technical claims and external references before publishing.
